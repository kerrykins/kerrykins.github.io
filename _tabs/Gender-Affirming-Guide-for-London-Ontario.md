{: .prompt-info }

For Transgender Awareness Month 2026, I decided to compile a comprehensive list of resources for my trans siblings so that there is a centralised spot to browse for resources. In addition to the already-amazing [*Gender Affirming Resources Ontario* spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/111o63Tvt20heohe9KtRW_AZPBKOz4kpZ1pb7r0CJpvU/edit?gid=1664012296#gid=1664012296), my guide focuses on London, ON specific resources. 

Writing and compiling this has been as much for others (I hope) as it is for me. Starting my transition has been exceptionally lonely and alienating for me. However, there is a lot of resources out there, and I'm hoping that others will know there is a wealth of resources and support for this journey. 

~Kerry
